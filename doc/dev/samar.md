# Samar — Dev Log (Branch: `samar`)
- **Design System:** Retro Warm Cream sharp-edge theme & QSS (`#ECE7DF`, `#4A3980`) in `styles.py`.
- **Main Window:** PySide6 `MainWindow` with `QStackedWidget` managing 5 core views.
- **Recording & Audio:** `AudioRecorderWidget` with live `WaveformWidget` and transcription preview.
- **Views & Components:** Note cards, transcript editor, AI summary/tasks, semantic search, and analytics.
- **Dialogs:** Multi-format export (PDF/DOCX/MD/TXT) and user profile/AI settings dialogs.
- **Config & Assets:** App bootstrap (`main.py`, `config.py`, `pyproject.toml`) and demo screenshots.
